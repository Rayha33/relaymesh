# Contributor covenant

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid
sharing private information. Maintainers may remove abusive, threatening,
discriminatory, or disruptive content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants who violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed in confidence.
